To my wonderful Mum,

Happy Birthday, Mum.

It's your birthday again and even tho your not here to receive a hug or home cooked food know your loved. I'm really grateful for you in my life, from the time I was young, you played with me , you cared you taught me and most of all you loved me. I'm sorry for the times I hurt you , you are really my best friend and better yet the best mum ever. I'm not just saying it because it's your birthday but because you inspire me, you give hope you assure me and even believe in me when I don't believe in myself.

You have never judged me but have guided me through life up until now. You have experienced a lot , good times and bad, it usually hurts knowing you went through all that you have, despite it all look how far you have come and how your family loves you.

I want you to be happy, happy to be around and see me grow, and to live your best life. We have one life and I wouldn't change anything about having you in mine. Take pride as you turn older that you have a daughter you have raised right, who sometimes you have small quarels with because she can be a bit hot headed and outspoken but she loves you so much , beyond words. You have set a great example for her about hard work, mindset , people skills, family, God and so much more. That daughter is me. I'm proud of you mummy I'm proud and honoured to call you my mother. I wish you many more years as my bestie and the best mum ever .

With love , From Bella.

With all my love,

Your loving child
